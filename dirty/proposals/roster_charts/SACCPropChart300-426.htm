  Voting: rules adopted in May 2002:                          
                                                 
  1.  A quorum constitutes all but two members [subsequently boosted to all but three with growth of committee size].   Abstentions do not count towards the quorum [non-English-first members automatically abstain on votes on English names, although their comments are solicited and posted]. 
                           
                                                 
  2. To pass, a proposal must receive more than two-thirds of the votes received,
once quorum reached.  As long as a proposal can still receive more than 3 NO
votes, it remains unresolved.
