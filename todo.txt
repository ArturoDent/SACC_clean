    <p>2. Sibley &amp; Monroe (1990) and Payne (1997) considered <i>Coccyzus pumilus</i> and <i>C. cinereus</i> to form
    a superspecies. They were treated in a separate genus, <i>Micrococcyx</i>, by Ridgway (1916) and Pinto (1938).
    Peters (1945) merged <i>Micrococcyx</i> into <i>Coccyzus</i>, and this has been followed in all subsequent
    classifications. However, Sorenson &amp; Payne (2005) and Hughes (2006) found that inclusion of these two species
    in <i>Coccyzus</i> would make that genus paraphyletic with respect to <i>Piaya</i>. Sorenson &amp; Payne (2005)
    also found strong support for a sister relationship between <i>Piaya minuta</i> and these two species, and
    treatment of these three in a separate genus, <i>Coccycua</i>, is the only way that <i>Coccyzus</i> and
    <i>Piaya</i> can remain monophyletic. <span class="info">SACC </span>proposal<span class="info"> passed to
      recognize <i>Coccycua</i>.</span> Banks et al. (2006) recognized <i>Coccycua</i>.</p>
