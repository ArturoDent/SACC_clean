## Follow-up: 18 linked, 29 intentionally left alone

`experiments/rosterMissingLinks/src/experiment_1.js` fills in the 18 genuine
gaps (no trailing `*` in `missingList.txt`) with a link to
`http://www.museum.lsu.edu/~Remsen/SACCprop{number}.htm` (bare number for
lettered sub-parts, e.g. `261a` → `261`), and now skips the other 29
(`*`-marked) entries entirely, per the correction above.

**Linked (18):** `6`, `187`, `216`, `217`, `225`, `270`, `315`, `478`,
`505`, `541`, `752`, `765`, `772`, `865B`, `930`, `91`, `1032`, `1052A`.
Two needed one-off source-defect repairs rather than a plain new link:
entry `91` already had a link but with a typo'd URL (`SACCrop91.htm`,
missing the second "p"), and entry `315` had malformed nested `<i>` tags
that needed fixing before its target phrase was a clean, wrappable run.
Entries `216`/`217` share a combined write-up page (see below) rather than
having their own.

**Intentionally skipped, unchanged (29):** `184`, `189`, `194`, `202`,
`209`, `210`, `211`, `214`, `215`, `226`, `244`, `258`, `259`, `261a`,
`261b`, `268`, `341`, `352`, `354`, `373`, `374`, `421B`, `440`, `441`,
`462`, `506`, `575`, `583`, `632`.

Side note from investigating before the `*` convention was known: several
of the skipped numbers (`209`, `210`, `211`, `214`, `215`, `373`, `374`)
turned out to have no individual page at all — live or in any Wayback
Machine snapshot (checked via the CDX index, ~30,800 archived URLs under
`~Remsen/`) — because the museum site only ever published them as
**combined write-ups covering several proposal numbers at once**:
`SACCprop209-211.htm`, `SACCprop214-218.htm` (also covers `216`, `217`, and
`218`), and `SACCprop373+374.htm`. That combined-page mapping is exactly
why `216` and `217` (the two non-`*` members of that same bundle) could
still be linked, even though their `*`-marked siblings `214`/`215` are
correctly skipped.

## Method

Parsed `clean/proposals/roster_charts/SACCproproster.htm` with jsdom, matched
every `<p>` whose text starts with a number/letter pattern (`\d+[A-Za-z]?\.`),
and checked whether it contains any `<a href>` pointing at a `SACCprop*`
page. 1,116 total numbered entries found; 69 had no such link. No changes
made — this is a report only.
